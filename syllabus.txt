On completion of this Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning course, students will be able to apply the knowledge of engineering, data science fundamentals, and artificial intelligence to solve complex engineering problems. Students will identify, formulate, and analyze problems to create solutions using the first principles of mathematics, statistics, and data science. The course develops foundational knowledge of computing theory, algorithms, and programming paradigms.

Furthermore, the curriculum covers digital logic design, including fundamental logic gates, Boolean algebra, combinational circuits, and microprocessor fundamentals. Students will also learn Python for logic development, implementing linear algebra functions for arrays using NumPy, and demonstrating high-performance data manipulation using the Pandas library. Data visualization will be performed using Matplotlib and Seaborn libraries. Finally, students must communicate effectively, adopt ethical practices, demonstrate an ability to lead projects, and adapt to changing software paradigms.

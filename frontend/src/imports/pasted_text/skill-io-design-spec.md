ABOUT THE PRODUCT

Skill/IO is an AI-powered education platform that helps students bridge the gap between university curriculum and industry expectations.

Instead of simply recommending courses, Skill/IO analyzes a student's syllabus, identifies missing industry skills, and creates personalized learning roadmaps, projects, interview preparation, and career guidance.

The product should feel like an AI operating system for students.

DESIGN STYLE

Create a Paper UI Design System.

Everything should feel like premium textured paper instead of plastic or glossy surfaces.

Use:

• Soft paper textures
• Warm neutral colors
• Thin borders
• Rounded corners (18–24px)
• Frosted paper glass effects
• Very subtle shadows
• Calm spacing
• Large whitespace

Avoid:

❌ Neon
❌ Bright gradients
❌ Overly colorful interfaces
❌ Glass that looks like Windows Vista
❌ Material Design look
❌ Heavy skeuomorphism
